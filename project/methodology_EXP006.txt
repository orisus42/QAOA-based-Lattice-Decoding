EXP-006: aggressive phase pruning on the same 64 toy Module-LWE examples
Internal research record, not final poster text or captions.

Purpose and design
  The user requested 75% and90% pruning after seeing EXP-004/005. These levels
  are an adaptive development extension, not a new confirmatory holdout.
  A100% endpoint was added as an explicit control: all pair interactions are
  removed, leaving independent single-qubit operations and a product sampling
  distribution. This control can be sampled classically without a QPU.
  The primary comparison retains the saved full-circuit global angles. A
  secondary comparison retrains one shared pair per pruning level on the same
  16 public training examples, disjoint from all64 evaluation matrices.
  Fields, normalization, original objective, encoding, mixer, initial state,
  16 qubits and one QAOA layer are unchanged. No reference optima or secrets
  enter training or the deterministic weakest-coefficient selection rule.

Individual losses and controls
  With frozen angles,75% improves single-shot optimum probability on43/64,
  but19/64 lose more than5%, with worst retention49.01% (50.99% loss).
  At90%,22/64 lose more than5%, with worst retention46.71%.
  Among the11 Babai failures,75% gives better expected distance than full QAOA
  on7 and worse on4;90% gives better on4 and worse on7. The53 already optimal
  Babai answers remain unchanged by the incumbent-retaining policy.
  Retraining reduces but does not eliminate individual losses: at both75% and
  90%,16 examples still lose over5% in single-shot optimum probability.
  The100% control is substantially worse on every instance's standalone
  optimum probability. Its128 new distributions agree with a direct product
  formula to numerical precision. This supports the usefulness of some pair
  interactions within our chosen circuit family, not necessity of entanglement
  for solving the problem or superiority over classical methods.
  Classical1/2-coordinate refinement still solves61/64 (95.31%) with mean
  distance2.685261, better than all tested QAOA hybrids in this ideal study.
