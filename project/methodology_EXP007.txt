EXP-007: frozen methods on 512 fresh toy Module-LWE lattice groups
Internal research record, not final poster prose or captions.

Prospective design
  D-018 records the configuration and its SHA-256 before cohort generation.
  This is a local timestamped analysis plan, not external preregistration.
  Seeds 2026102400 through 2026102911 specify 512 independent examples, each
  with its own polynomial matrix A, secret and error. The generator uses the
  same n=2, k=2, q=17, eta=2 distribution as the earlier development study.
  This is an eightfold increase in example count, not an increase in dimension.
  Expanded A modulo q identifies public lattice groups. All new groups must
  be distinct and absent from the 64 development and 16 training matrices.
  The runner aborts on an overlap rather than replacing or screening examples.
  No case is excluded for difficulty, noise, Babai success, or box coverage.

Frozen methods and controls
  Full fixed QAOA and 75% pruning retain EXP-003 shared angles.
  The 90% method retains the global angles trained in EXP-006 on the original
  16 training cases. No fresh example is used for fitting. A secondary 75%
  method likewise retains its EXP-006 globally trained angles.
  All methods retain p=1, 16 qubits, the same initial state/mixer, full objective
  and original normalization, and the [-2,-1,0,1] Babai-centered coefficient
  box. Pruning deletes the weakest floor(fraction * nonzero terms), with
  lexicographic ties, from the phase operator only.
  Public solver inputs exclude planted secrets, errors, and exact references.
  References certify global CVP via bounded secret enumeration with optimal
  modular lifts; they are used only for evaluation. Missing box optima count
  as failures instead of causing a new box or sample selection.
  Keep Babai's answer unless a sampled candidate has strictly lower full cost.
  Compare with greedy one- and two-coordinate classical refinement (up to
  1024 candidate evaluations), uniform box sampling, and CBD secret sampling.
  CBD uses the known small-secret prior and can propose points outside the box.

Measurements and validation
  Every QAOA distribution is computed independently by NumPy and by Qiskit/Aer
  after all-to-all compilation into rz/sx/x/cx. The exact distributions must
  agree. Analytic p=1 full-objective expectations supply another check.
  Independently seeded NumPy categorical draws from the verified distribution
  simulate 16384 measurements for every method and example. These are ideal
  simulation samples, not actual QPU measurements or repeated hardware runs.
  Prefixes give budgets 1, 256, 1024, 4096, 8192 and 16384. All draws are saved.
  Exact expectations, rather than a fortunate sampled batch, drive comparisons.
  Increasing shots changes best-of-budget success, not single-shot probability.
  Direct original-basis costs verify each returned candidate. New cost-PMF
  expectations are checked against the older independent order-statistic code
  at both 1024 and 16384 shots, including expected distance itself.

Analysis fixed before outcomes
  The primary budget is 1024, matching earlier work. The two primary candidates
  are 75% with unchanged angles and 90% with previously retrained angles.
  Define extra benefit as mean(Babai distance - expected hybrid distance).
  The primary quality criterion is retention of at least 95% of full QAOA's
  extra benefit. Compute a paired bootstrap over the 512 independent groups
  (20000 replicates). Require the 2.5th percentile of the retention ratio to
  exceed 0.95, rather than accepting a favorable point estimate alone.
  These are approximate one-sided 97.5% bounds, allowing Bonferroni accounting
  for two primary retention claims. The bootstrap is not an exact coverage
  guarantee. Other metrics/budgets/75%-trained are secondary, without a blanket
  multiplicity-adjusted superiority claim.
  Report exact-optimum probabilities, strict improvements, mean distance and
  squared distance, outcomes conditional on Babai failures, individual losses,
  observed sample outcomes, planted-secret recovery and compiled resources.
  Paired bootstrap intervals quantify instance variability under this generator.
  Poisson-binomial prediction intervals separately quantify shot variability
  on this fixed cohort. These describe neither hardware noise nor larger sizes.

Limits
  Tiny Module-LWE-style algebra is preserved; this is not a full ML-KEM KEM,
  cryptanalytic attack, demonstration of scaling, or proof of quantum advantage.
  The extra distance includes the secret-length term, as in previous runs.
  Gate counts exclude routing and device noise. No QPU time or credits are used.
  If these fresh outcomes motivate new method choices, further independent data
  will be needed to evaluate those new choices without adapting to this cohort.

Classical context and observed samples
  One/two-coordinate refinement solves 497/512 (97.0703%), with 129/129/513
  minimum/median/maximum public candidate evaluations. CBD secret sampling plus
  Babai gives 97.0507% exact success at 1024 samples. Both exceed all the QAOA
  hybrids at that budget. At 16384, QAOA hybrids can exceed the deterministic
  refinement's success, using many more candidates; CBD and QAOA differ by
  quality metric and support. Equal sample counts are not equal computational
  effort, and there is no end-to-end quantum advantage result.
  Observed hybrid exact counts at 1024 are 460,458,457,462; at 16384 they are
  511,510,507,511. These finite sampled batches fluctuate and do not replace
  the exact expectation comparison. For example, 75% same-angle has a lower
  observed count than full at 1024 despite a higher expected success rate.

Interpretation and next step
  The primary 75% resource/quality trade-off carries over to this larger fresh
  cohort. Its previously trained variant is a promising secondary candidate.
  The 90% method remains a smaller-circuit option with a less reliable quality
  trade-off; it should not be described as equivalently validated.
  Next evaluate backend routing, noise and usage estimates for a small paired
  full-versus-75% hardware study before spending any of the 600-second budget.
